About the studio

The Waypoint Studio is a small, independent shop making cozy printable coloring books and craft-ready designs. Our books are built for calm, low-pressure coloring: one friendly scene per page, thick lines and plenty of room for your own colors. We also design SVG and PNG cut files for Cricut, sublimation and laser crafters.

Everything we sell is available through our Etsy shop, where you can read reviews and download your files right after purchase.
