Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Waypoint Studio ("we", "us", "our") handles information on this website, in our online shop, and in the internal tool we use to manage our own Pinterest account through the Pinterest API.

1. Who we are

The Waypoint Studio is a small independent business based in the United States that designs printable coloring books and digital craft files. Our products are sold through our shop on Etsy at thewaypointstudio.etsy.com.

2. Information we collect

This website. This website is informational only. It has no accounts, forms, shopping cart or advertising. We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and links people use, so we can improve the site. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as pages visited, links clicked, approximate location (city or region), device and browser type; it does not store full IP addresses. Links from this site to our Etsy shop include tracking labels (such as utm_source=website) so we can see how many visits the site sends to the shop. You can opt out of Google Analytics by blocking cookies in your browser or with Google's opt-out add-on at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) for security and reliability. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts are requested.

Customer orders. When you buy from our Etsy shop, Etsy shares the information needed to fulfill your order, such as your name, email address and, for physical items only, your shipping address. We use it only to deliver your purchase, answer your questions and meet legal and tax requirements. Physical items are produced and shipped by our print partner, Printify, which receives your shipping details for that purpose only. Payments are handled by Etsy and we never see your card details.

3. How we use the Pinterest API

We use the Pinterest API through an internal tool that only we use. The tool connects to our own Pinterest business account so that we can:

  • create, schedule and organize Pins of our own products on our own boards; and
  • view analytics (such as impressions, saves and clicks) for our own Pins and boards, so we can see which products interest people.

The tool does not offer sign-in to other Pinterest users, and we do not access, collect or store data about other Pinterest users' accounts, boards or Pins.

Pinterest-derived data. Data we receive from the Pinterest API is limited to our own account's boards, Pins and analytics. We use it only to plan and improve our own Pinterest content. It is kept only as long as we need it for that purpose and is then deleted. We access the API through a secure third-party integration service and use AI assistant tools to help plan and draft our Pins; these services process the data only to carry out our requests.

We do not sell, rent, resell or redistribute Pinterest content or Pinterest-derived data to any third party, and we do not use it for advertising profiles or combine it with other data to identify individuals.

This website and our app are not endorsed by, sponsored by or affiliated with Pinterest. Pinterest is a trademark of Pinterest, Inc.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell or share personal information. We only share it with the service providers described above (Etsy, Printify and our integration tools) as needed to run our shop, or when required by law.

5. Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, at any time by contacting us as described below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights, and we will honor them.

7. Contact us

For any privacy questions or requests, email us at thewaypointstudiopa@gmail.com or message us through our Etsy shop: thewaypointstudio.etsy.com (use the "Message" button on the shop page). We usually reply within one business day.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with its effective date.
